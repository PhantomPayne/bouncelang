## How to Read This Log

- **Finding IDs** use the format `Rn-Fm` (Round n, Finding m). Self-review findings during a round
  use the suffix `-review` (e.g., `R2-F3-review`).
- **Categories** are: Guess, Friction point, Spec gap, Ambiguity, Contradiction.
- **Impact checkboxes** may have multiple boxes checked if multiple impacts are possible.
- **Recommendation type hints** are suggestions for the recommender agent — they may be overridden
  after a fuller analysis.
- **The final summary** (§§A–E) is the primary handoff to `spec-recommender-agent.md`.
